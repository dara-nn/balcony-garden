# Image credits

Every file in this folder was downloaded and committed here, nothing is hotlinked.
This is a personal garden log, not a commercial site. Where a photo is a shop's or
a nursery's own product photography, that is stated plainly: it is reused here to
show what the plant or the crop looks like, with the source named. No licence is
claimed for those beyond that. Creative Commons and public domain files carry their
real licence and author.

## Note on the three raspberry pots

Maurin Makea, Takalan Herkku pot 1 and Takalan Herkku pot 2 each get their own six
files, three whole plant and three crop, with no file shared between them. The whole
plant photos are all generic potted raspberries: no Finnish seller photographs the
potted plant itself, they all photograph the berries.
